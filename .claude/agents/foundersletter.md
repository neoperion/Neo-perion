1–2 — UI & UX problems
Drop-cap large initials (T and N in blue) are inconsistently applied — only 2 paragraphs have them with no pattern. Either apply consistently to every section start or remove all.
On mobile (375px), the letter content column is almost full-width — there's essentially no margin. Reading long-form text at full viewport width on mobile causes line lengths of 60+ characters per line, reducing readability.
The co-founder credits at the bottom (Adhi Ganesh K, Tamilselvan) are very small text — they deserve equal, prominent credit as co-founders.
No clear CTA after the letter — a motivated reader who finishes this letter has nowhere to go. This is a missed conversion moment.
The blockquote styling (italic, blue left border) is strong — but the quote is too long. The best pullquotes are 1–2 sentences, not a full paragraph.
