# Neo Perion V2.0 - Homepage Specification

This document serves as the single source of truth for the entire Neo Perion V2.0 homepage.

## Core Goals
By the end of the homepage, the visitor must understand:
- **Who we are:** Neo Perion Solutions
- **What we do:** AI-Powered Product Engineering
- **Who we help:** Startups, SMBs, Education, Healthcare
- **Why trust us:** Process + Expertise + Technology
- **Next Step:** Book a Free Consultation

## Page Structure
1. Hero
2. Tech Ecosystem
3. Industries
4. Services
5. Why Neo Perion (Bento Grid)
6. Process (Timeline)
7. Case Studies
8. Testimonials
9. CTA

---

## 1. Hero Section
- **Headline:** Transforming Ideas into Enterprise-Grade AI
- **Subheadline:** We build scalable digital products, AI solutions, and SaaS platforms that position your business for the future.
- **Primary CTA:** Book a Free Consultation
- **Secondary CTA:** Explore Our Work
- **Trust Indicators:** "Trusted by forward-thinking startups and enterprise leaders."
- **Visuals:** Three.js V1 Canvas (Nodes, Connections, Floating particles ONLY. No complex 3D models).
- **Animation Rules:** Fade-in and slight slide-up on load. Do not over-animate.

## 2. Tech Ecosystem (Marquee)
- **Technologies:** React, Next.js, Node.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Supabase, Tailwind CSS, Google Gemini, OpenAI, AWS, Azure, Google Cloud.
- **Visuals:** Transparent SVG logos with uniform padding.
- **Scroll Speed:** Slow, continuous, infinite loop.
- **Hover Effects:** Pause on hover, slight brightness increase on the hovered logo.

## 3. Industries Section
- **Education:** Scalable learning management systems and AI tutors.
- **Startups:** Rapid MVP development and scalable architectures.
- **SMB:** Digital transformation and business process automation.
- **Healthcare:** Secure, HIPAA-compliant patient portals and telehealth.
- **Layout:** 4-column grid or large staggered cards. Subtle glassmorphism.

## 4. Services Section
Highlighting our core offerings:
1. **Product Development:** End-to-end SaaS and enterprise product engineering.
2. **Web Development:** High-performance web applications using modern tech stacks.
3. **Mobile Development:** Native and cross-platform mobile experiences.
4. **Artificial Intelligence:** Custom LLM integrations and machine learning pipelines.
5. **Advanced AI:** Specialized model fine-tuning and predictive analytics.
6. **Business Automation:** Streamlining operations with custom internal tools.
7. **Startup Support:** Fractional CTO services and technical guidance for founders.

## 5. Why Neo Perion (Bento Grid)
- **Content Focus:** Enterprise architecture, transparent communication, rapid iteration, and future-proof tech stacks.
- **Layout:** Asymmetric Bento Grid.
- **Visuals:** Glassmorphism used specifically on these cards (not the whole background). Subtle hover gradients.

## 6. Process (Timeline)
- **Steps:** 
  1. Discovery & Strategy
  2. Architecture & Design
  3. Agile Development
  4. Testing & QA
  5. Deployment & Scaling
- **Visuals:** Vertical timeline or numbered progressive steps.

## 7. Case Studies
- **Format:** Featured high-impact projects.
- **Content:** Client name, industry, challenge, solution, and impact metrics (e.g., "500% increase in throughput").

## 8. Testimonials
- **Format:** Clean carousel or masonry grid.
- **Content:** Client quote, name, role, company.

## 9. CTA Section
- **Headline:** Ready to build the future?
- **Subheadline:** Let's discuss your next big idea.
- **Primary Action:** Book a Free Consultation
- **Visuals:** Clean, focused gradient background. No distracting elements.

---

## Risk Mitigation & Rules for Sprint 2
- **Risk 1 (Three.js Hero):** Stick to V1 (Nodes, Connections, Particles). No massive models.
- **Risk 2 (Overusing Animations):** Motion must improve hierarchy. Avoid random fades/scales.
- **Risk 3 (Glassmorphism Abuse):** Use glass ONLY on Cards, Bento Grid, and Floating Elements.
- **Risk 4 (Homepage Length):** Strictly adhere to the 9-section structure above. Do not add filler sections.
